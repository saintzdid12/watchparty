WatchParty

Watch several Twitch streams side by side, with chat, in a clean macOS-style app.

Download for Windows Portable
Windows 10 and 11
  • Grid or SpotlightEvery stream the same size, or one big with the rest beside it. Click a small one to swap it in.
  • One sound at a timeHear the stream you pick, and a white ring shows which one it is. Number keys 1–9 switch.
  • One tidy panelNow listening, your streams, live follows, popular channels and groups. Chat takes its place when you open it.
  • Full windowCtrl+B and the streams fill the whole window, edge to edge.
If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
